About The Company

We believe that every person has a story, and those stories are worth being told well. Whether it is a large scale production, or the digitizing and restoration of your precious family moments - everything gets the same level of care, craft, and consideration. Trinity Arts Group serves as a platform for the work of many talented artists who share our shared vision of a just and equitable world—and the artist's role in it. We believe art speaks when words fail us.

Film Transfer Services

We offer high-definition video transfers of most existing tape formats, preserving your precious memories and historical content with professional quality.

Photo Restoration

Professional photo restoration services to bring new life to damaged or aged photographs, preserving family history and important moments.

A $5.00 per-tape discount is applied to orders of ten or more. However, there is no discount on film negative transfers due to the age of materials and the labor involved.

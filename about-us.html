Who We Are

Patrick Flynn

Founder & Creative Director

Patrick Flynn is a seasoned video editor, writer, and filmmaker with over a decade of experience in broadcast and digital journalism, narrative and documentary features, and new media. His writing and directing credits include the theatre pieces "For Every Evil Under the Sun" and "John Cassavetes' Husbands" (an Official Selection of the 2010 "Under the Radar" Festival), as well as "Servers" (an Official Selection of the 2013 undergroundzero festival). His documentary work includes the shorts "Joan", "Dig a Hole to Put the Devil In" and the feature "Our Hometown". He is currently working on the live audio drama, "The Hard Fall", which will be premiering in 2026.

Rebecca Meszaros

Producer & Voice Talent

Over the span of her 20+ year career in broadcasting, she has worked many freelance jobs writing, voicing, and producing commercials for radio. She also hosted, produced, and assisted in creating five separate podcasts. She was a longstanding instructor at the Connecticut School of Broadcasting from 2005-2020 when the school shut down due to Covid-19. During that time, she also executive produced "The Bruce Williams Show" for Lifestyle Talk Radio while being promoted to Assistant Operations Manager of that station, working with 35 different radio talk shows ranging from news, financial advice, open forum, and many others. From 2008-2011 she was a freelance producer for Dr. Judy Kuriansky on Z100 FM. She currently is a traffic reporter for both CBS and 1010WINS.

Tohm Bakelas

Poet, Musician & Filmmaker

Tohm Bakelas is a social worker in a psychiatric hospital. He was born in New Jersey, resides there, and will die there. His poems have been printed widely in journals, zines, and online publications all over the world. He is a recipient of the 2023 Northwind Writing Award (Raw Earth Ink). He is the author of twenty-seven chapbooks and several collections of poetry, including "Cleaning the Gutters of Hell" (Zeitgeist Press, 2023) and "The Ants Crawl in Circles" (Bone Machine, Inc., 2024). He has read his poems across the United States and England. He runs Between Shadows Press.

Our History

Trinity Arts Group has its name rooted not only in our proud Irish traditions, but in the former New Jersey pipefitting and contracting business Trinity Plumbing & Heating, which was ran for over forty+ years by Founder Patrick Flynn's father, Tim. In doing so, we hope to honor his legacy of quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and always - pride in one's work. Céad Míle Fáilte!

Current Projects

Discover our latest creative endeavors and storytelling projects

“The Hard Fall"

Envisioned as an eight-episode true crime audio drama about our broken criminal justice system and the lengths a man will go through to prove his innocence. It's a tale of banal bureaucratic indifference, stalwart determination, and an inspiring love story born through conversation. As the entire legal apparatus of the State attempts to silence him, his wife, and his legal team; a network of unlikely allies — including a disaffected juror and a recanted eyewitness emerge.

This project attempts to demystify the whole entire judicial process by utilizing legal and law enforcement experts, archival interviews, and scripted scenes with actors to achieve some semblance of the truth. It is our goal to humanize the story of Prisoner #59554 in Rahway State Prison: V. James Landano, and how ironclad his resolve truly became in the face of insurmountable odds.

Awarded 2025 grant by The Puffin Foundation

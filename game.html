“Red Harvest" Game Screen

With my deep knowledge of film and its history, I decided to envision a game I would have loved to see growing up in the 1990’s – an adaptation of the book that started an entire genre! Dashiell Hammett’s classic 1929 noir, "Red Harvest", and its enigmatic main character – known colloquially as ‘the Continental Op’. As of 2025, Hammet’s book and the “Op” character have fallen into the public domain
